Downtown Kalamazoo, quarter by quarter

Welcome. This site follows downtown Kalamazoo while the City rebuilds its streets: how many people visit, how much traffic comes and goes, how full the parking gets and how busy the bus stops are. Western Michigan University updates it every quarter for the City of Kalamazoo.

Trends since 2023

What the data shows

Traffic

Traffic has changed little

Apart from one road that was nearly closed for part of 2024, traffic into and out of downtown has stayed close to the same level from year to year.

Traffic in detail →
People

Millions of visits every quarter

Downtown draws visitors, the people who work here and the people who live here. Each group is tracked month by month.

People in detail →
Parking

There is room to park

Even at the busiest time of day, most of the counted parking spaces downtown are empty. Parking is usually considered full at around 85%.

Parking in detail →
Transit

The Transit Center handles most bus activity

Most people getting on or off a bus downtown do so at the Kalamazoo Transit Center rather than at a street stop.
