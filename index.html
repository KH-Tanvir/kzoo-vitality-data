Trends since 2023

What the data shows

Traffic is flat, and the 2024 dip was one road. South Westnedge Avenue nearly emptied from March to October 2024. As the City noted at the time, that traffic was diverted to Burdick, Oakland and Portage rather than lost.

Parking is never close to full. Around a third of the counted spaces are in use at the weekday afternoon peak, and less in the evenings.

The Transit Center is the bus network downtown. Close to nine in ten boardings and alightings happen there; street-stop activity fell mainly because one stop, Rose at Water, stopped being served in late 2024.

Compare visits with care. The study area was redrawn after the City's Q3-2024 feedback, and visit figures step up in January 2025 as a result. The year-over-year columns in the source data compare like with like; the raw totals do not.
