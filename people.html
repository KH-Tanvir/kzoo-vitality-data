People downtown

Trips into the downtown study area each month, split into people who live there, work there, and everyone else. A visit is a trip, not a person: one person can make many.

Read year-to-year change from the source's own comparison, not from the totals. The study area was redrawn after the City's Q3-2024 feedback, so visit counts before and after January 2025 do not cover the same ground. The chart below uses the year-over-year field supplied with the data, which compares like with like.

Notes

Visits add up, people do not. A quarter's visits are the sum of its months. Unique people and dwell time are monthly averages, because the same person appears in more than one month.

Dwell time shifts in January 2025 along with the boundary change: visitor dwell drops from about 186 to 130 minutes, while resident and employee dwell rise. Treat it as a break in the series, not a change in behaviour.

Panel visits are not published here. The source's raw device sample grows two to four times over the period as the panel grows, which makes it useless as a trend. It stays in data/people_monthly.csv for reference.
