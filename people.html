People downtown

Trips into the downtown study area each month, split into people who live there, work there, and everyone else. A visit is a trip, not a person: one person can make many.

Comparing years. From January 2025 the figures use an updated study-area boundary, so counts before and after that date cover slightly different areas and should not be compared directly. To see how visits have really changed, use the chart below: it compares each quarter with the same quarter a year earlier, over the same area.

How to read these figures

Who is who. Residents are people estimated to live in the study area, employees are people estimated to work there, and visitors are everyone else. The figures are estimates built from anonymous mobile-phone location data.

Visits and people. A visit is one trip into the study area. The same person can come many times, so visits are always higher than the number of people.

Adding up a quarter. A quarter's visits are the total of its three months. The number of people, visits per person and time spent are monthly averages, because the same person usually appears in more than one month.

Time spent downtown. The average time per visit also shifts in January 2025 with the new boundary — shorter for visitors, longer for residents and employees — so compare it within each period rather than across the change.
