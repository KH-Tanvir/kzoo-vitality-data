Traffic

Average vehicles per day on the nine main roads into downtown, counted separately toward downtown (incoming) and away from it (outgoing).

The 2024 dip is one road. Incoming traffic on South Westnedge Avenue fell from about 9,500 vehicles a day to roughly 230 between April and September 2024. The City reported that this traffic was diverted to Burdick, Oakland and Portage, not lost. Choose that corridor above to see it.

Notes

What a quarter means here. Each month holds an average day's traffic, so a quarter is the average of its three months, not a sum.

Seasonal shape. Six of the nine corridors peak in September. West Michigan Avenue runs about a quarter below its yearly average in May, June and July and well above it in September, which follows the Western Michigan University calendar.

Source. StreetLight network performance exports, run monthly by the study team under the City's subscription.
