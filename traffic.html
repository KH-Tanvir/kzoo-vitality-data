Traffic

Average vehicles per day on the nine main roads into downtown, counted separately toward downtown (incoming) and away from it (outgoing).

Why traffic dipped in 2024. During roadwork, traffic heading into downtown on South Westnedge Avenue almost stopped: about 230 vehicles a day from April to September 2024, against 9,500–12,000 a day in the same months of 2023. Drivers used other routes instead, mainly Burdick Street, Oakland Drive and Portage Street. Oakland and Portage are among the nine roads counted here, and both got busier. Burdick is not counted, so the total fell even though the trips still happened. Choose South Westnedge Avenue in the menu above to see the change.

How to read these figures

An average day. Each monthly figure is the number of vehicles on an average day that month. A quarter's figure is the average of its three months.

In and out. Incoming means heading toward downtown and outgoing means leaving it, so a round trip is counted once in each direction.

Busy and quiet months. September is the busiest month on most of these roads. West Michigan Avenue, the main route to Western Michigan University, is about a quarter quieter than usual from May to July, while students are away, and much busier in September.
