About the study

The City of Kalamazoo is rebuilding a dozen downtown streets over twelve years under Streets for All. Western Michigan University is measuring what happens to downtown while that work goes on.

The study tracks eleven measures of downtown economic vitality every quarter, from June 2024 through December 2026, using a base year of 2023. This site carries the four measured datasets: traffic, visits, parking and transit. The quarterly reports add business and resident surveys, property occupancy and travel time.

Study area
About one square mile of downtown. The boundary was refined in 2024, which affects visit figures from January 2025.
Reporting
A report each quarter, roughly six weeks after the quarter ends, with a one-page summary for downtown businesses.
This site
The study's data record: every monthly figure behind the reported metrics, updated each quarter.

Questions about the study, the surveys or the data: contact the research team at Western Michigan University.
