About the study

Downtown Kalamazoo is in the middle of one of the largest public works projects the City has undertaken. This study measures how downtown life and business hold up while the work goes on.

Under Streets for All, the City of Kalamazoo is rebuilding about a dozen streets in and around downtown over twelve years: returning one-way streets to two-way traffic and improving routes for walking, cycling and transit. To follow the effect on downtown, the City asked Western Michigan University to measure its economic vitality every quarter from mid-2024 to the end of 2026, against a 2023 starting point.

This site shows the four measures counted every month — traffic, visits, parking and bus use. The study's quarterly reports add surveys of businesses, residents and visitors, commercial property figures and travel times.

Study area
About one square mile of downtown Kalamazoo. The boundary was updated at the start of 2025.
Reporting
A full report every quarter, about six weeks after the quarter ends, with a one-page summary for downtown businesses.
