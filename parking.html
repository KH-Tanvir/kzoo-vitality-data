Parking

A count of parked vehicles in the city's counted facilities, taken each month on a weekday afternoon, a weekday evening and a weekend evening. Occupancy is vehicles divided by spaces.

Nothing here comes close to full. Parking studies commonly treat 85% as effectively full, the point where drivers start circling. The highest single month in this data is about 41%.

Notes

What is counted. The city's counted public facilities only — not private lots, and not every space downtown. Each month is a single count in each time window, not a continuous measurement.

How the headline figure is built. Each facility type is its occupied spaces divided by its spaces. The figure for a time window is the average of the three types, and the quarterly figure is the average of its months. The study's reports use the same method.

Small differences from the published reports. These figures are recalculated from the counts, so they run about a point above the 2026 report figures: the reports keep Lot 20's 36 spaces in the denominator after that lot stopped being counted. Earlier quarters differ where a report formula averaged the wrong months. The data page lists each difference.

Source. City of Kalamazoo occupancy counts, compiled with Discover Kalamazoo.
