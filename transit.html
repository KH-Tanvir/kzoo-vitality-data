Transit

People getting on and off buses at the downtown stops. These are passenger movements, not people: a rider who transfers at the Transit Center is counted twice.

Most bus activity downtown is at the Transit Center. Close to nine in ten boardings and alightings at downtown stops happen at the Kalamazoo Transit Center. Street stops have been quieter since late 2024, mostly because the stop at Rose and Water Street closed for road construction in November 2024.

How to read these figures

Boardings and alightings. A boarding is someone getting on a bus and an alighting is someone getting off. The figures count these movements, not individual riders: someone who changes buses at the Transit Center is counted getting off and getting on again.

Stops closed for construction. The stops at Rose and Water Street and at Rose and Butler Street have been out of service since November 2024 because of road construction. Other stops have paused for construction at times since 2024 and then reopened.

Two sides of the Transit Center. The center has stops on Rose Street (stop 1004) and Burdick Street (stop 1011). From April 2026 much of the activity moved to the Burdick Street side, while the total at the center barely changed.

Months with no figure. If a stop has no figure for a month, it was not in service, and it is left out rather than counted as zero.
