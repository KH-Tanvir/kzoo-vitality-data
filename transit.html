Transit

People getting on and off buses at the downtown stops. These are passenger movements, not people: a rider who transfers at the Transit Center is counted twice.

The Transit Center is the network downtown. Close to nine in ten ons and offs happen at stops 1004 and 1011. The fall in street-stop activity is mostly one stop: Rose at Water, which has no recorded activity after October 2024.

Notes

Ons + offs, not riders. The study's reports label this "transit users", which overstates it: a round trip counts at least twice, and so does a transfer.

Service changes move these numbers. Stop 1 (Rose at Water) and stop 1079 (Rose at Butler) have been out of service since November 2024. Several Westnedge-area stops went quiet in August and September 2024 during construction and returned in 2025.

The Transit Center switched sides in spring 2026. Activity moved from the Rose side (stop 1004) to the Burdick side (stop 1011) from April 2026, while the combined total barely changed. The two sides are labelled inconsistently in the source workbook, so this site reports them by stop number.

A blank month is not a zero. Where a stop has no figure, it was not reported or not served, and it is left out of the calculation rather than counted as nothing.

Source. Kalamazoo Metro (K-Metro).
